## 💼 **Journey User Flow**

### **Typical User Path:**

**Profile:** Sarah, owner of a 5-person software consultancy

**Step 1 - Health Check:**
- Revenue: $800K/year
- EBITDA: $240K (30% margin)
- **Insight:** "Strong profitability for professional services"

**Step 2 - Unit Economics:**
- CAC: $2,500 (sales & marketing)
- LTV: $45,000 (3-year client lifespan)
- LTV:CAC: 18:1
- **Insight:** "Exceptional unit economics - invest more in acquisition"

**Step 3 - Revenue Forecast:**
- Current: $67K/month
- Growth: 15%/year
- 12-month projection: $873K
- **Insight:** "Revenue will grow to $873K with current trajectory"

**Step 4 - Cash Flow:**
- Current cash: $120K
- Monthly burn: $15K
- Runway: 8 months
- **Insight:** "Adequate runway, but watch AR aging"

**Step 5 - Pricing:**
- Current rate: $150/hour
- Cost: $75/hour (50% margin)
- Market: $175/hour
- **Insight:** "Can increase rates 15% and still be competitive"

**Step 6 - Break-Even:**
- Fixed costs: $400K/year
- Contribution margin: 50%
- Break-even: $800K revenue
- **Insight:** "Already profitable at current scale"

**Step 7 - Valuation:**
- EBITDA: $240K
- Multiple: 4.5x (consulting industry)
- Valuation: $1.08M
- **Insight:** "Business worth ~$1M, can grow to $1.5M+ with improvements"

**Journey Outcome:**
Sarah now knows her business is worth $1M, has strong unit economics, and can increase pricing 15% to accelerate growth to $1.5M valuation.
