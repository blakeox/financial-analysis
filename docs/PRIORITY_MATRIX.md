# Priority Matrix for Platform Improvements

**Date:** December 2024  
**Purpose:** Guide development priorities based on impact vs effort

---

## 🎯 Decision Framework

### Impact Score (1-10)
- **10:** Transformational - Changes the product category
- **7-9:** High - Significant user value or business impact
- **4-6:** Medium - Noticeable improvement
- **1-3:** Low - Nice to have, marginal benefit

### Effort Score (1-10)
- **1-3:** Low - Few hours to few days (Quick wins ✅)
- **4-6:** Medium - Days to weeks
- **7-9:** High - Weeks to months
- **10:** Very High - Months+ effort

### ROI = Impact ÷ Effort
